# Competitors — Who Owns What, What They Leave Open

Distilled from: Blue Ocean Rev 2 + Competitor Gap Analysis (July 2026).
Use for: differentiation copy, objection handling, "why not X" sections, partnership/content strategy.

**The through-line:** everyone owns a **vehicle** (podcast, Substack, funnels) or a **mindset/healing**. Nobody owns **the message itself**. Chloe leaves every one of them still sounding like everyone else.

## Faith lane (Layer 1)

| Competitor | Owns | Leaves open | Strategic read |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stefanie Gass** (God-Led Business / Podcast to Profit) | Podcasting as vehicle; "grow without social"; clarity-on-calling; multi-7-figure; skews 30s–40s moms | The craft of the message. Her "clarity" is niche/tagline-level. No AI, no DFY. Her own students post "my offer isn't selling" AFTER her clarity work | Study, don't fight. Her engine (long-form content → email → one clear offer) is the validated blueprint for this no-social audience. Her graduates = downstream referrals |
| **Erin Harrigan** (Redefining Hustle) | Theology of ambition — surrender, identity, anti-hustle; 45–60 incl. empty-nesters (closest demographic) | ALL marketing mechanics: no messaging, copy, visibility, AI, DFY. "Her clients know their assignment; many still can't articulate it to a stranger" | Proves the older established Christian woman is a reachable market. Richest pool of DFY-budget prospects. Podcast guest spot = priority. (She has a Bernedoodle; the booking writes itself) |
| **Nicole Frazier** (Level Up Life & Co) | "Mindset & Messaging Coach" branding — the vocabulary overlap | Every deliverable is mindset (EFT stacks $47–127, $375 sessions, 1:1). "Messaging" = confidence about your message; she never produces words. 30s–40s, Instagram-centric | The overlap is vocabulary, not offer. Her clients leave with courage and no About page — pre-warmed for DFY. Her tagline stops where Wanda's business starts |
| **Haley Henderson** (The Well Ministries) | Discipleship-in-business; identity, stewardship; $47 Reset → membership → mastermind; 30s–40s | Nothing on messaging, copy, content, visibility, AI. "Her women finish grounded — and still facing a blank page" | **Partner, not competitor.** Aligned theology, zero deliverable overlap, warm attendees who just re-consecrated businesses and need words |
| **Kristin Dronchi** (Kingdom Lens / Kingdom Substack System) | The Substack channel, loudly: "Substack as front-end trust pathway," trademarked frameworks | Voice extraction and AI. Her "message" work is positioning/movement-level; nobody leaves with their own sentences | The one to watch on the shared channel. Define the boundary BEFORE launch: she teaches WHERE to build and WHAT movement to lead; Wanda teaches HOW to sound like yourself once there. A Substack membership must never read as a Dronchi derivative |
| **Ashley June** (Set-Apart Online Business) | Funnels, scaling, 6–7-figure framing; high-energy | The 55–65 buyer's register — her hype voice pattern-matches to what FTC-literature-reading buyers distrust | The gap she leaves: Truth Over Hype for the skeptical, seasoned woman |
| **Sandra Mosley** (Fempreneur Chronicles) — *independent sweep find* | Faith+AI stewardship, "AI without erasing your God-given voice," newsletter-first (Beehiiv), 35–55 | Messaging-specific DFY, voice-first system, 55–65 focus, offer ladder. She's the permission layer ("Is AI sinful?") just upstream of Wanda's lane | **Closest anyone gets to the frame. Collaborator before competitor** — build the warm relationship now; she's the most likely drift into the lane |

## Secular adjacents (Layer 2 — where Chloe gets stranded)

| Competitor | What they sell | Where Chloe is left |
|---|---|---|
| **Alex Cattoni** (Copy Posse Launch Pad, $2,997) | Becoming a copywriter | At the door — she doesn't want a career, she wants her copy to work. Gap: the "write it for me, don't retrain me" market |
| **Marie Forleo** (B-School ~$2K–2.5K, now + AI tools) | Generalist online-business education | Breadth when she needs depth, hype when she needs safety. **Forleo adding AI in 2026 is the starting gun, not competition** — the mainstream is normalizing AI; the faith lane has no trusted messaging voice on it |
| **Joanna Wiebe** (Copyhackers / Copy School) | ~1,000 hours of pro copywriter training; drifting enterprise | Respected and overwhelmed. Wanda = the translation layer: Copyhackers-grade craft delivered as a finished product |
| **Amy Porterfield** (post-DCA) | DCA RETIRED (2025 final cohort); now Milly Club ($500K+ founders) and Made to Scale ($1M+) | **The beginner she served is orphaned.** The "built it, launched it, nobody bought" woman — almost always a messaging failure — has no guide. Direct intake channel (rec #10) |
| **Faith-AI tools** (Faith AI, FaithGPT, Beyond Creations) | SaaS content generation / custom GPTs for ministries | Tools, not messaging strategists. Volume, not voice. Their existence = permission structure: the market normalized "AI + faith"; Chloe now needs someone to TRUST inside it |
| **Secular brand-voice AI** (Atom Writer, Junia, Hoppy Copy, You Bots) | Voice replication from 20 clean samples | Every one needs her voice as INPUT. Wanda extracts the voice she can't see herself. Name them defensively in copy so Chloe never wonders "why not a $27 tool?" |
| **Midlife coaches** (Rosenstein, EvolveMe, Second Act Success) | Life/reinvention/confidence for 50–65 | They help her decide to start and feel brave. Nobody translates 25 years of proven career into online words. Audience already primed and spending |

## Vacated mainstream lane

**Christy Wright** (Business Boutique / Ramsey) left in 2021, wound down the brand. Her 2016–2021 audience is now aging directly into 55–65 **with no heir**. Her material was start-oriented: no messaging depth, no AI. The mainstream lane has a Wanda-shaped opening at the messaging-and-AI end. (Runner-up guess if the name matters: Ruth Soukup.)
