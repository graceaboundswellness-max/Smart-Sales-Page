# Content Topic List — Built From the Research

Every topic below is receipt-backed: it comes from a mapped search territory (`open-lanes.md`) or a strategic recommendation (`positioning.md`). Priority order reflects the research's own flags (⭐ fastest wins = weakest incumbents), not guesswork.

Each topic lists: the target query → the AEO question a real person types → the angle that wins (the gap) → working titles.

## Tier 1 — Fastest wins (write these first)

**1. ⭐ Template fatigue** — `why copywriting templates don't work`
- AEO question: "I bought so many templates and swipe files — why does my copy still sound generic?"
- Angle: literally NOBODY answers the buyer's side (the only strong incumbent argues the copywriter-gatekeeping side). Root cause: voice clarity must exist before any template can work.
- Titles: "I Bought All the Templates. Why Does My Copy Still Sound Like Everyone Else's?" · "Why Copywriting Templates Don't Work (Until You Do This First)"

**2. ⭐ Feeling invisible** — `feel invisible in my business`
- AEO question: "I'm great at what I do — why don't more people know about me?"
- Angle: nearly undefended SERP; results route to therapists and workplace content. Bridge the emotional experience to the business diagnosis. Use Wanda's owned word (SILENCE) and Get Off Mute.
- Titles: "Great at What You Do, Invisible Online: The Real Reason (It's Not Marketing)" · "Why Nobody Knows About Your Business — a Diagnosis, Not a Pep Talk"

**3. ⭐ Called but exhausted** — `christian woman burnout business`
- AEO question: "I know God called me to this business — so why am I so exhausted by it?"
- Angle: page 1 is podcasts, Facebook posts, and a 7,850-word transcript. Every incumbent assumes employees to delegate to. Write to the woman solopreneur, answer up front.
- Titles: "Called to This Business and Completely Worn Out — What's Actually Wrong" · "When the Calling Is Real but the Exhaustion Is Too"

**4. Christian AI ethics (most winnable HIGH-VALUE territory)** — `is it wrong for Christians to use AI in business`
- AEO question: "Is it wrong for a Christian to use AI to write her marketing?"
- Angle: the only audience-matched incumbent is a low-authority newsletter, and she's compounding — move now. Nobody combines the stewardship answer + a practical voice-first method + authority. This is the "Your voice first. AI runs with it" cornerstone.
- Titles: "Is It Wrong for Christian Women to Use AI in Business? An Honest Answer" · "AI Without the Ick: A Christian Woman's Rule for Using AI Without Losing Her Voice"

## Tier 2 — Core positioning territories

**5. Brand voice discovery** — `how to find your brand voice`
- Angle: every incumbent says CONSTRUCT a voice (adjectives, charts). The gap = "you don't create a brand voice, you RECOVER the one you already have." This is Wanda's positioning stated as a content thesis.
- Title: "You Don't Need to Find Your Brand Voice. You Need to Recover It."

**6. AI in your own voice** — `how to make ChatGPT sound like me`
- Angle: Reddit is #1 on both phrasings; the #2 expert paywalls his answer. Answer in the first 3 sentences, show the extraction method (not "feed it 20 samples").
- Title: "How to Make ChatGPT Sound Like You (When You're Not Even Sure What You Sound Like)"

**7. Messaging misdiagnosis** — `why is my business not getting clients`
- Angle: nobody structures it as a DIAGNOSTIC ("here's how to tell WHICH problem you have"). Matches Wanda's topic name exactly.
- Title: "Not Getting Clients? Here's How to Tell Which Problem You Actually Have"

**8. One-liner / explaining what you do** — `how to explain what you do`
- Angle: SERP fractured across three intents; nobody owns the business-owner lane. Get Off Mute is the purpose-built answer — include finished one-liner examples in the reader's mouth (no incumbent does).
- Title: "'So… What Do You Do?' — How to Answer in One Sentence Without Freezing"

**9. Over-50 online business** — `starting an online business at 55`
- Angle: everyone anchors on 50; "55" is nearly free. UGC owns the top → Google wants first-person lived experience. Five rebuilds = the unreplicable credential.
- Titles: "Starting an Online Business at 55: What I Know After Rebuilding Five Times" · "Is It Too Late to Start at 57? (Asked and Answered by Someone Who Did)"

**10. Salesy vs serving** — `how to sell without being salesy`
- Angle: nobody anywhere serves Christian women entrepreneurs selling their own offers; faith results target men or employed salespeople. Truth Over Hype as the method.
- Title: "How to Sell Without Being Salesy — for the Christian Woman Who'd Rather Not Sell at All"

## Tier 3 — Channel & authority builders

**11. Visibility without social media** — `grow a business without social media`
- Angle: faith lane 100% empty; a DEAD sales page ranks page 1. Answer "is it even possible?" in sentence one.
- Title: "Yes, You Can Grow a Business Without Social Media. Here's the Faith-Friendly Version."

**12. Substack for experts** — `how to grow on Substack`
- Angle: low domain-authority barrier, zero answer-first content, #2 paywalls half the answer. Real numbers from Wanda's own Substack as receipts.
- Title: "How to Grow on Substack When You're an Expert, Not an Influencer"

**13. Email opens** — `why is nobody opening my emails`
- Angle: disambiguate in the title (marketer intent); position 2 is held by unedited AI output. Root-cause structure.
- Title: "Why Nobody's Opening Your Emails (It's Not the Subject Line)"

**14. Hiring a coach safely** — `how to vet a business coach`
- Angle: nobody addresses the reader ALREADY burned — trust after losing $5K, shame, discernment vs. fear. Faith angle: zero incumbents.
- Title: "How to Vet a Business Coach After You've Already Been Burned"

**15. AI Overviews for small experts** — `how to show up in Google AI Overviews small business`
- Angle: every winner writes for local SMBs/B2B; nobody writes for the solo expert / coach / personal brand.
- Title: "How to Show Up in Google's AI Answers as a One-Woman Business"

## Rules for using this list

1. One topic = one territory = one primary query. Don't blend territories in a single post.
2. Before writing, re-read that territory's entry in `open-lanes.md` — build to the GAP, not the generic topic.
3. Every post ends with Research Receipts (see CLAUDE.md / project instructions).
4. Field data is July 2026 — if using this list after ~October 2026, re-check the target SERP before investing in a post.
